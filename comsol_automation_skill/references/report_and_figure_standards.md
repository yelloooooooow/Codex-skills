# Report and Figure Standards

Reports should include:

- Objective.
- Inputs and sources.
- Model structure.
- Geometry, physics, mesh, and solver settings.
- Exported outputs.
- Verification/validation status.
- Assumptions and limitations.
- Reproducibility commands.

Figures should have units, legends, captions, and data provenance. Avoid overclaiming from a single curve or local field snapshot.
