# Terminology

- Domain: COMSOL region where equations are solved.
- Boundary: lower-dimensional entity where boundary conditions apply.
- Selection: named entity group used to stabilize model automation.
- Feature: COMSOL node under geometry, physics, mesh, study, solver, or result.
- Derived value: COMSOL evaluation output such as global, average, integration, or probe values.
- Dry-run: execution plan without COMSOL mutation or solve.
