# Reporting Standard

## Final Report Structure

Use:

1. Abstract or executive summary.
2. Introduction and objective.
3. System/model description.
4. Methods and assumptions.
5. Results.
6. Optimization/economics/calibration sections as applicable.
7. Discussion.
8. Limitations and uncertainty.
9. Conclusions and future work.

## Methods

State model scope, governing assumptions, solver settings, case definitions, data sources, and validation/calibration approach.

## Results

Use figures and tables with explicit units. Interpret trends physically. Do not overclaim precision when inputs are reconstructed or estimated.

## Discussion

Explain tradeoffs, sensitivity, feasibility, and why selected optima are useful.

## Required Assets

- final result summary CSV;
- figure list;
- table list;
- key equations;
- figure explanations;
- video explanations;
- final audit report and checklist.

## Limitations

Separate missing original data, model simplifications, numerical assumptions, calibration uncertainty, and economic uncertainty.
