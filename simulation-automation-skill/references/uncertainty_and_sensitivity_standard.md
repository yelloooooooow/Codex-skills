# Uncertainty and Sensitivity Standard

## Missing Data Register

Maintain a register with:

- missing or uncertain item;
- source/location;
- impact on results;
- recovery method;
- adopted value;
- confidence;
- need for manual confirmation.

## Data Classification

Label values as:

- directly from source/literature;
- digitized or reconstructed;
- engineering estimate;
- calibrated;
- optimized;
- user-provided.

## Sensitivity Topics

Include sensitivity for:

- uncertain material properties;
- viscosity and thermal expansion in CFD;
- boundary conditions;
- mesh/time-step settings;
- cost assumptions;
- energy price;
- efficiency/COP correlations;
- control gains or initial conditions.

## Reporting Rule

Never present assumptions, estimates, or calibrated values as if they were original source data.
